# Online Class Quality Criteria Checklist (v2.0)

*Note: Screen sharing is a standard baseline across all tiers. Overall quality is evaluated through instructional delivery, student engagement, pedagogical technique, and time management.*

---

## Quick Reference Summary

| Tier | Core Descriptor | Primary Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Excellent** | High engagement, fully structured, student-centered | Satisfies all 4 stages of the instructional flow plus Socratic questioning. |
| **2. Good** | Direct, purposeful, disciplined, practice-driven | Efficient transitions, domain precision, minimal downtime ($\ge 95\%$ time utilization). |
| **3. Average** | Functional but passive, paced unevenly | Operational friction, extended dead air, or unbalanced participation. |
| **4. Poor** | Neglectful, unprepared, structurally inadequate | Camera off, hostility, instructional abandonment, or zero preparation. |

---

## 1. Excellent

A high-engagement, fully structured session that balances positive rapport with rigorous, student-centered learning.

### Primary Observed Points

* **`exe01` Complete 4-Stage Flow:**
  1. *Welcoming check-in / rapport:* Personal icebreaker, student baseline check.
  2. *Interactive warm-up:* Quick tactical recall or puzzle activation.
  3. *Structured concept delivery:* Step-by-step introduction of the core topic.
  4. *Supervised application:* Hands-on gameplay/exercises with real-time coaching.
* **`exe02` Active Visual Presence:** Coach maintains a live, clear camera presence with an energetic, supportive demeanor throughout the session. **[Video]**
* **`exe03` Socratic Facilitation:** Prompts deep understanding through guided questioning and scaffolding (*"What happens if black plays...?"*) rather than simply providing answers. **[Transcript]**
* **`exe04` Structured Closure:** Formally recaps key takeaways, assigns independent work clearly, and manages class administration/onboarding. **[Transcript]**

### Extra Classification Criteria

* **`exe05` Dynamic Visuals:** Uses dynamic on-screen annotations, board arrows, and highlights to focus student attention. **[Video]**
* **`exe06` Comparative Analysis:** Systematically explores and compares multiple candidate solution paths before validating the correct approach. **[Transcript]**
* **`exe07` Equitable Voice Balance:** Actively balances floor time to ensure equitable participation across vocal and quieter learners. **[Transcript]**

---

## 2. Good

A purposeful, disciplined, and practice-oriented session with clear direction and minimal downtime.

### Primary Observed Points

* **`gd01` Punctual & Direct Transition:** Minimal informal delay; transitions straight into the core lesson within the opening 1–2 minutes. **[Transcript]**
* **`gd02` Systematic Step-by-Step Questioning:** Methodically calls on students in turn to break down complex tasks into logical increments. **[Transcript]**
* **`gd03` Domain Precision:** Enforces exact subject-matter terminology, clear definitions, and proper notation. **[Transcript]**

### Extra Classification Criteria

* **`gd04` High Time Utilization:** High scheduled time utilization ($\ge 95\%$) with no rushed or neglected sections. **[Transcript]**

---

## 3. Average

A functional but passive class hampered by low energy, over-reliance on media, or pacing issues.

### Primary Observed Points

* **`avg01` Media Dependency:** Relies heavily on pre-recorded third-party media or videos to deliver the core concept instead of teaching directly. **[Transcript / Video]**
* **`avg02` Extended Dead Air:** Frequent 3–5 minute stretches of silence during tasks without teacher check-ins, hints, or intervention. **[Transcript / Video]**
* **`avg03` Operational Drag:** $\ge 5$ minutes of instructional time lost to technical setup, broken tournament links, login issues, or tool permission troubleshooting. **[Transcript]**
* **`avg04` Weak Follow-Through:** Dismissive or indifferent attitude toward homework review. **[Transcript]**

### Extra Classification Criteria

* **`avg05` Monopolized Attention:** Allows one or two dominant students to answer everything while others drift into passive silence. **[Transcript]**

---

## 4. Poor

An unacceptable session marked by poor preparation, lack of presence, and instructional neglect.

### Primary Observed Points

* **`pr01` Instructor Camera Inactivity:** Instructor keeps camera turned off while expecting or having students remain on camera. **[Video]**
* **`pr02` Zero Lesson Preparation:** Joins late or unprepared, openly asking students what has or has not been taught. **[Transcript]**
* **`pr03` Instructional Abandonment:** Leaves students unattended to attend to personal calls or exits the session before activities end. **[Transcript]**
* **`pr04` Passive "Babysitter" Media:** Streams generic instructional videos without direct teaching, commentary, or synthesis. **[Transcript / Video]**
* **`pr05` Content Mismatch & Dismissiveness:** Assigns material completely misaligned with the cohort's level and rejects student questions bluntly. **[Transcript]**

### Extra Classification Criteria

* **`pr06` Unannounced Tardiness:** Unannounced significant tardiness or uncommunicated delays. **[Transcript]**
* **`pr07` Hostility or Sarcasm:** Hostile, sarcastic, or dismissive communication toward struggling students. **[Transcript]**

## Excerpt & Demo Evaluation Protocol

When assessing transcripts that represent snippets or trial sessions rather than full standard classes:

1. **Duration Check ($< 5\text{ minutes}$):** Mark conditionally as **"On Track for [Tier]"** based strictly on the opening stage observed. A definitive grade requires instructional delivery and wrap-up.
2. **Immediate Capping (Red Flags):** Any occurrence of `pr01`, `pr02`, `pr07`, or severe `avg03` in the initial segment caps the maximum possible session grade at **Average** or **Poor**.
3. **Demo Discovery Check:** Verify whether the instructor proactively uncovers student background (age, standard, previous training, baseline board knowledge) prior to problem solving.
